# Match-Up Assignments (Play-Twice Opponents)

Each team plays **6 opponents twice** (and the remaining 12 once). The "twice" opponents fall into three categories:

- **\* Regular**: Fixed rivalry — always played twice regardless of ladder position
- **^ Assigned**: Same ladder bracket at end of previous season (including finals results)
- **& Conditional**: Required when a Regular opponent already falls in the same bracket, freeing up a slot that must be filled by another team
