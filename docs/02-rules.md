# Rules

## Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Game** | A single match between two teams |
| **Round** | A scheduling block (all games in one round happen in the same week) |
| **Rematch** | The second time two teams play each other in a season |
| **Pair** | Two interstate teams from the same state |
| **Streak** | Consecutive Home or Away games; a Bye does NOT reset a streak |
| **Acceptable** | Does not break an Absolute Rule |
| **Permissible** | Allows flexibility, relates to Preferential Rules |

An opponent restriction expressed as a list is exhaustive unless it explicitly says otherwise. A locked match, bye, venue or broadcast slot must be retained; a `NOT LOCKED` match-up is fixed but its displayed time and venue may be changed if needed.

## Absolute Rules

### Home & Away Allocation

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| A1 | Every team must play exactly **12 Home** and **12 Away** games, and have exactly **3 Byes** |
| A2 | No team can play more than **3 consecutive Away games** |
| A3 | No **Interstate team** can play more than **2 consecutive Away games**, UNLESS separated by a Bye (e.g. Away, Bye, Away, Away — or — Away, Away, Bye, Away) |
| A4 | No **Victorian team** can play more than **2 consecutive Away games against Interstate teams**, UNLESS separated by a Bye. It IS acceptable for a Victorian team to play 3 consecutive Away games against other Victorian teams |
| A5 | No **Victorian team** can play more than **2 consecutive games at interstate locations**, UNLESS separated by a Bye. (Relevant because of Gather Round) |
| A6 | No team can play **2 consecutive Away games against both WA teams** (Fremantle and West Coast), UNLESS separated by a Bye over 3 rounds (e.g. Away, Bye, Away) |

### Byes

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| A7 | All byes must be separated by **at least 2 games** between occurrences |
| A8 | No team can have its **first Bye after Round 16** |
| A9 | No team can have **all 3 Byes before Round 16** |

### Opponents / Match-Ups

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| A10 | Each team plays 6 opponents twice and 12 opponents once. The "twice" opponents are determined by: **Regular** (fixed rivalries), **Assigned** (same ladder bracket), and **Conditional** (when a Regular opponent falls in the same bracket, requiring an extra double-up) |

### Rematches

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| A11 | Minimum **5-round separation** between rematches (games against the same opponent) |
| A12 | A team **cannot host the same opponent twice** (if you play them twice, one is Home, one is Away) |

### Playing Days

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| A13 | All teams must have a minimum **5 days off** (not counting match days) between consecutive games |

### Venues

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| A14 | No more than **one game** at the same venue on the same day |

## Preferential Rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| P1 | For each **Pair** (same-state interstate teams), when both are playing in a round, one should be Home and the other Away |
| P2 | **Interstate teams** should alternate Home and Away from week to week where possible |

## Streak Clarification

A Bye does **not** reset a streak. A streak is the number of consecutive Home (or Away) games before a game of the opposite type. Maximum streak = 3. Byes are not counted when measuring consecutive games.

## Gather Round Special Rule

For **Gather Round (Round 6)** only — all games are played in South Australia:
- Only **Victorian teams** can be the Home team at a non-primary/secondary ground
- For Victorian teams, a Home game in Gather Round is **treated** as an Away game at a ground outside Victoria for Rules A4 and A5, but remains a Home game for the 12 Home / 12 Away tally.
