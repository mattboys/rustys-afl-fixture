## MCG Priority Rules

All of the following match-ups **must** be played at the MCG, regardless of either team's primary ground:

1. **Carlton** vs any MCG-primary team (Collingwood, Hawthorn, Melbourne, Richmond)
2. **Essendon** vs any MCG-primary team (Collingwood, Hawthorn, Melbourne, Richmond)
3. **Carlton vs Essendon** (always at MCG)

## Collingwood Docklands Obligation

- Collingwood must play **2 of its 12 Home games** at Docklands Stadium
- These 2 games **cannot** be against: Carlton, Essendon, Hawthorn, Melbourne, or Richmond

## Venue Sharing (Same-Day Conflicts)

- Two teams whose primary ground is **Docklands Stadium** may both play Home on the same day, provided one of them has the MCG as a secondary ground and uses it (playing against an MCG-primary or Interstate team)
- Two teams whose primary ground is the **MCG** may both play Home on the same day only if each uses a currently permitted Home venue. The documented Docklands option is Collingwood's contractual allocation, and it must be against a Docklands-primary or Interstate team; it cannot contradict the MCG priority rules above.
