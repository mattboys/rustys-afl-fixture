# Home & Away Game Rules (Detailed Clarifications)

These clarifications define Home and Away treatment and venue eligibility.

## Section 1: Home & Away Games

1. **Home game** = Team A hosts Team B at Team A's Primary Ground, an applicable Secondary Ground, or an expressly permitted contractual or Gather Round venue
2. **Away game** = Team B is hosted by Team A
3. Every match has exactly **one Home team** and **one Away team** — never both Home, never both Away

## Section 2: Primary & Secondary Grounds

### Interstate teams with NO secondary ground
Adelaide, Port Adelaide, Brisbane, Fremantle, West Coast, Sydney — can only play Home games at their single Primary Ground.

### Interstate teams with secondary grounds

**Gold Coast**: 6 of 12 Home games at secondary venues:
- 4 at Marrara Oval (Darwin) — Rounds 11, 12, 13, 22
- 2 at Riverway Stadium (Townsville) — Rounds 17, 18

**Greater Western Sydney**: 4 of 12 Home games at Manuka Oval (Canberra):
- Rounds 8, 13, 22, plus 1 unassigned round

### Victorian teams with secondary grounds

**Hawthorn**: 4 of 12 Home games at Cazaly's Stadium (Cairns) — Rounds 11, 12, 13, 22

**Melbourne**: 4 of 12 Home games at Traegar Park (Alice Springs) — Rounds 11, 12, 13, 22

### MCG rule for Carlton & Essendon
When Carlton or Essendon plays as the **Home team** against any of Collingwood, Hawthorn, Melbourne, or Richmond → the match must be at the **MCG**. Also, Carlton vs Essendon is always at the MCG. It still counts as a Carlton/Essendon Home game.

### Collingwood's Docklands obligation
2 of 12 Home games at Docklands Stadium. These **cannot** be against: Carlton, Essendon, Hawthorn, Melbourne, or Richmond.

Docklands Stadium is an expressly permitted contractual Home venue for Collingwood; it is the exception to the ordinary primary/secondary-ground rule.

## Section 3: Consecutive Away Games (Detailed)

### Interstate teams (Rule A3)
- ❌ 3 Away games across 3 consecutive rounds
- ✅ Away, Away, Bye, Away (3 away in 4 rounds)
- ✅ Away, Bye, Away, Away (3 away in 4 rounds)

### Victorian teams (Rule A4)
- ❌ 3 Away games *at grounds outside Victoria* across 3 consecutive rounds
- ✅ 3 Away games *at grounds inside Victoria* across 3 consecutive rounds
- ✅ Away (interstate), Away (interstate), Bye, Away (interstate) in 4 rounds
- ✅ Away (interstate), Bye, Away (interstate), Away (interstate) in 4 rounds

### Streak definition
A **streak** = consecutive Home or consecutive Away games before a game of the opposite type.
- Maximum = 3
- A **Bye does NOT reset** a streak
- Byes are not counted when measuring consecutive games

## Gather Round (Round 6) — Special Treatment

- All games played in South Australia
- Only **Victorian teams** can be the Home team at a non-primary/non-secondary ground
- For Victorian teams, a Home game in Gather Round is **treated as** "an Away game at a ground outside of Victoria" for consecutive-away tracking (Rules A4, A5)
- It is still **counted as** a Home game (for the 12H/12A tally)

## Superseded Ground Data

No supplementary-ground allocation applies. Only the primary, secondary and contractual venue requirements documented in this specification are current.
